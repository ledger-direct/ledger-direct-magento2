# Images and texts in this folder

The pictures under `images/` and the article texts in `catalog.json` are **not** covered by the
repository's MIT licence. All rights reserved; they may be used only to demonstrate LedgerDirect, in
the try-it shops of the LedgerDirect plugins and the images built from them.

The pictures are computer-generated renders of fictional characters. The articles are demo articles in
a shop on the XRP Ledger testnet: nothing is sold or shipped.

The source of both is `TryoutCatalog`, maintained outside this repository; `sync.py` copies it here.
